# Code of Conduct

CRASR projects adopt the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as the code of conduct for all repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces.

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, welcome people of all backgrounds and experience levels, and keep feedback focused on the work.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the repository maintainers privately, or reach CRASR through the [center's contact page](https://crasr.artsandsciences.baylor.edu/contact-us). Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with discretion.

Members of the Baylor community are also subject to Baylor University policies.
